# Customer Support Service Levels

Critical production outages receive an initial response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day. High-priority issues receive an initial response within four business hours. Normal requests receive an initial response within one business day.

If a critical issue is not acknowledged within 30 minutes, the support platform pages the on-call engineering lead. The hosted analytics service has a monthly availability target of 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.
