# Security and Audit Policy

## Audit log retention
Application audit logs are retained for 365 days. Security incident records are retained for seven years after closure unless a longer period is required by law. Audit logs are access-controlled and immutable after ingestion.

## Access management
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for production systems. Access is granted using least privilege and reviewed quarterly. Privileged access requires an approved ticket and is removed within one business day after role change or termination.

## Incident response
A suspected security incident must be reported to Security Operations within one hour of discovery.
